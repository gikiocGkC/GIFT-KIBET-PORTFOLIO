WHO I AM

Creative work.
Clear communication.

I am a multimedia and communications professional with a background in media and digital communication.

At the Open University of Kenya, my work sits within Corporate Communications and Marketing. I contribute to the development of visual materials, institutional publications, event photography and video, social media content, and multimedia learning resources for digital course delivery.

I am particularly interested in the point where design and communication meet: making information easier to understand, giving stories a strong visual structure, and producing content that works across different formats and platforms.

My approach is practical and audience-aware. I consider the purpose of a communication piece, the information it needs to carry and the way people will encounter it before deciding how best to present it.
